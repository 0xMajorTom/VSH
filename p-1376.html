Reflecting on 2024 Achievements and Shaping 2025 Goals

At Vulcan Shield Hungary, 2024 was nothing short of transformative. It was a year that saw us pushing the boundaries of innovation, expanding our capabilities, and achieving significant milestones that have strengthened our position as a global leader in alumina fiber research, development, and production. From groundbreaking advancements in product performance to the establishment of new manufacturing capabilities, we have not only addressed the evolving needs of our customers but also set new benchmarks for the high-performance materials industry.

As we step into 2025, we remain steadfast in our commitment to delivering cutting-edge solutions that empower industries to tackle their most challenging high-temperature applications. We take immense pride in sharing our key achievements from the past year and unveiling our ambitious plans for the future—plans that will further enhance the capabilities of alumina fiber materials and expand their applications across aerospace, petrochemicals, energy, electronics, and beyond.

With innovation at the heart of everything we do, VSH is poised to lead the charge in creating sustainable, high-performance materials that not only meet but exceed industry expectations. Join us as we reflect on our successes in 2024 and outline the exciting initiatives that will shape the next chapter of our journey.

2024 Achievements: Pioneering New Heights in Alumina Fiber Technology

1. Breakthroughs in M-99 Continuous Fiber

M-99, our alumina fiber with 99% alumina content, underwent transformative improvements in 2024. One of the greatest challenges we faced was enhancing its strength and modulus, key performance indicators that our customers across industries had eagerly anticipated. After countless trials and extensive R&D efforts, we succeeded in:

  • Improving the strength and modulus of M-99 to meet and exceed industry expectations.
  • Transforming M-99 into woven fabric and unidirectional fabric, unlocking its potential for a wide array of applications across industries such as aerospace, energy, and advanced composites.

These advancements are a testament to our dedication to innovation and customer-centric development.

2. The Only Enterprise in the World Capable of Manufacturing both Long and Short Alumina Fibers

One of our proudest achievements in 2024 was the opening of our state-of-the-art manufacturing plant in Guangxi. This milestone makes Vulcan Shield Hungary the only enterprise in the world capable of manufacturing both long and short alumina fibers.

  • Enhanced Customization: Whether long or short fiber is required, VSH provides tailored solutions for diverse applications, from thermal insulation to composite reinforcement.
  • Increased Production Capacity: With this facility, we can meet growing global demand while maintaining the highest quality standards.
  • Cost Efficiency: Streamlined production processes and economies of scale translate into competitive pricing for our customers.

The addition of our plant in Guangxi plant solidifies VSH’s position as a global leader in alumina fiber manufacturing, ensuring our customers have access to reliable, cutting-edge materials for even the most demanding applications.

3. Expanding Our Global Footprint

In 2024, we at Vulcan Shield Hungary expanded our reach to serve industry leaders in aerospace, petrochemicals, and advanced materials across the USA, Germany, Malaysia, Indonesia, and beyond. By building relationships with top-tier clients, we’ve strengthened our role as a trusted supplier of high-performance materials in critical industries.

2025 Goals: Innovating for Sustainable and High-Performance Solutions

1. Researching New Applications

One of our primary goals for 2025 is to explore how VSH’s alumina fiber can revolutionize applications in the 3C (Computer, Communication, Consumer Electronics) industries. With a focus on lightweight, high-strength, and thermally efficient materials, we aim to support advancements in areas such as:

  • Thermal management systems for electronic devices.
  • Battery component insulation for next-generation energy storage solutions.
  • Electromagnetic shielding in consumer electronics.
2. Enhancing Product Specifications

Continuous improvement is at the core of VSH’s mission. In 2025, we plan to further refine the technical specifications of our long and short alumina fibers, including:

  • Higher strength and modulus to expand their use in advanced composites and extreme environments.
  • Enhanced thermal resistance for critical applications in aerospace energy sectors.
  • Improved flexibility and customization to meet the needs of diverse industries.


2025 will mark a significant milestone with the improvement and launch of our C-85 Alumina Fiber, which boasts 85% alumina content. This next-generation fiber will deliver improved performance metrics, including greater thermal stability and mechanical strength, making it an ideal solution for various high-temperature applications.

We are also working on transforming C-85 fibers into textile forms, such as woven fabrics and tapes, to meet the growing demand for customizable and versatile materials. These new forms will enable industries like petrochemicals, energy, and electronics to leverage the exceptional properties of C-85 fibers in innovative ways.

3. Improving Industry Applications

To solidify our position as a leader in high-temperature insulation, we’re actively researching new industry applications, including:

  • Petrochemical Refineries: Developing improved fired heater linings and catalyst insulation solutions.
  • Glass Manufacturing: Enhancing roller insulation and protecting furnace linings against wear and heat loss.
  • Electric Vehicles (EVs): Optimizing battery insulation and thermal shields for EVs and hybrid vehicles.
4. Strengthening Our Leadership in R&D and Awareness

VSH is committed to being a leader in alumina fiber research and development. In 2025, we will:

  • Invest further in R&D collaborations with various universities, research institutions and industry partners.
  • Develop innovative marketing campaigns to increase awareness of the transformative potential of alumina fiber in high-temperature applications.
  • Participate in global trade shows and conferences, such as Glasstec and the Global Petrochemical Conference, to showcase our expertise and connect with industry leaders.

Building the Future Together

As we move forward into 2025, Vulcan Shield Hungary remains dedicated to pushing the boundaries of innovation, sustainability, and excellence. By continually improving our products, expanding our applications, and forging stronger connections with customers, we aim to set new standards in the high-performance materials industry.

Whether you’re in aerospace, petrochemicals, energy, or manufacturing, VSH’s alumina fiber solutions are here to meet the demands of your most challenging environments.

Contact us today to learn more about our products and how we can collaborate to achieve your goals in 2025 and beyond.
