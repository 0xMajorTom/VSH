Vulcan Shield Hungary takes Flight at Singapore Airshow 2024!

Last week, Vulcan Shield Hungary was actively engaged at the Singapore Airshow 2024, utilizing the platform to foster vital connections within the aerospace industry. Against the backdrop of captivating exhibits showcasing turbines, engines, and various aircraft components, our team embarked on meaningful discussions with manufacturers and cable service providers. These conversations centered on potential collaborations harnessing the exceptional properties of Alumina Fiber, including its high-temperature resistance, lightweight high performance, corrosion resistance, and electrical insulation. Such attributes position Alumina Fiber as a prime candidate for enhancing the efficiency and durability of aerospace equipment.

In the bustling exhibition hall, we had the privilege to interact with renowned global aerospace brands such as Safran, Airbus, Boeing, and Thales, among others. These interactions provided invaluable insights into the industry’s evolving needs and reaffirmed our commitment to innovation and excellence in aerospace engineering.

Throughout the event, our interactions with distributors proved fruitful as we established links with factories and their teams, facilitating the seamless integration of Alumina Fiber into aerospace supply chains. In-depth discussions with R&D teams provided invaluable insights, reinforcing our commitment to innovation and excellence in aerospace engineering.

Reflecting on the past week, Vulcan Shield Hungary looks forward to further expanding its network within the aerospace industry. For those interested in joining us on this journey, we encourage you to reach out directly at david.kneale@vulcanshield.eu. Together, let’s continue propelling innovation to new heights in aerospace engineering.
