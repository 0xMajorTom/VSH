Introducing Vulcan Shield Hungary’s Alumina Fiber Needled Blanket

The NF-1600 alumina fiber needled blanket is manufactured using proprietary equipment through an entirely physical production process, containing no organic additives or binders. A double-sided needling technique strengthens inter-fiber cohesion, resulting in superior tensile integrity throughout the blanket structure.

Designed for demanding thermal environments, the NF-1600 delivers exceptional insulation performance without requiring controlled atmosphere conditions during use. It offers robust resistance to both high temperatures and chemical exposure, including acid and alkali environments. Rated to a classification temperature of 1600°C, the blanket retains its performance characteristics even under sustained exposure to extreme heat.

Our Alumina Fiber Blanket can be customised to meet Your Specific Needs

Fully customisable needled blanket based on your industry and application:

  • Thickness: 3mm to 100mm
  • Widths: 610mm / 1220mm
  • Lengths: 3.6m to 20m
  • Bulk density: 96kg/m³, 128kg/m³ and 150kg/m³.

]]> High-Temperature Resistant Alumina Bulk Fiber

M-F-72 alumina bulk fibers is a new ultra-lightweight high-temperature material. It is produced
using the “sol-gel” method, where a specially formulated spinning colloid is blown at high speed to form a fiber matrix, then dried and subjected to medium to high-temperature heat treatment to create polycrystalline fibers.

M-F-72 alumina bulk fiber offers exceptional thermal insulation properties without requiring special atmospheric protection. It can withstand high temperatures and resist acid and alkali corrosion, with a classification temperature of 1600°C. It maintains flexibility, softness, and fiber structure in oxidizing, neutral, or mildly reducing atmospheres at these high temperatures.
